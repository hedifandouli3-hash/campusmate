# CampusMate V8 — Business Model (B2B2C Institution-Funded)

## Core Principle
**Students and professors never pay for individual CampusMate subscriptions.**  
Universities, higher education institutes (e.g., ISET Béja), and training centers purchase an annual institutional license and provide CampusMate access to their verified students, professors, and staff.

## Primary & Secondary Revenue Streams
1. **Annual Institutional Licenses** (`University Plus`, `University Pro`, `University Ultra`) — primary B2B2C revenue.
2. **Company Recruitment Plans** (`Basic`, `Professional` 600 TND/yr indicative, `Enterprise` custom) — verified organizations recruiting interns/PFE students.
3. **Commercial Marketplace Services** — ordinary student listings remain 100% free; verified commercial sellers may purchase featured/pro listings.
4. **CampusMate Housing** (`/student/housing`, `/housing-provider`) — free for students; verified housing providers pay 20 TND / 30 days (single listing) or 150 TND / year (Pro Annual).
5. **Responsible Educational Sponsorships** (`/partners`, `/partner`) — clearly labeled contextual placements, separated from official RAG and toggleable per institution.
