## 6 End-to-End Hackathon Demonstration Scenarios

1. **Teacher Uploads & Publishes a Course:**
   - Log in as **Teacher** → `/teacher/documents` → Upload a `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.pptx`, or `.txt` file (or paste text), select section `TI2-DSI-A`, keep "Publier immédiatement" checked, submit.
   - Switch to **Student** → `/student/courses` & `/student/notifications` → The new course and notification appear immediately.
2. **AI Learning Hub:**
   - Open any course in `/student/courses/[id]` → Generate a **Summary** (5 modes), generate and take an **AI Quiz**, and ask a question in **Assistant Q&A** (returns grounded source + page citation).
   - Visit `/student/study-planner` → Generate a personalized **AI Study Plan** and toggle completed sessions.
3. **AI University Assistant:**
   - Open `/student/ai-assistant` (or the Dashboard quick widget) → Ask: `Comment obtenir une attestation d'inscription ?`
   - The assistant retrieves the sample ISET Béja procedure and provides a direct action button to `/student/documents`.
4. **Digital Administration:**
   - As **Student**, open `/student/documents` and submit a request for `Attestation d'inscription`.
   - Switch to **University Admin** → `/administration/document-requests` → Click **Approuver & Déposer Spécimen Démo**.
   - Return to **Student** → `/student/documents` → Download the clearly labeled unofficial demo specimen.
5. **Academic Group Communication:**
   - As **Teacher**, open `/teacher/chats`, write a message and check **Épingler comme annonce officielle du groupe**.
   - Switch to **Student** → `/student/chats` → See the pinned announcement and live group chat.
6. **Professional Opportunities & Internships:**
   - As **Organization**, open `/organization/internships/new` and publish an internship offer.
   - Switch to **Student** → `/student/internships` → View AI compatibility score, open `/student/cv-builder` to export PDF CV, generate an editable motivation letter, and apply.
   - Switch back to **Organization** → `/organization/applications` → Update status to **Convoquer en entretien** or **Accepter**; Student sees the update and notification.
